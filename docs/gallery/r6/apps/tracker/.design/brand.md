# Brand: Pigeonhole
Identity: a sorting office. Motif: **the pigeonhole** (inset slot with a brass key cap) repeats in: the case on the Desk, the key strip at the foot (each key sits in a slot), the spine's destinations (slotted), the toast (a slip dropped in a slot). Second motif: perforated tear line on every slip (record surface).
Voice: short, imperative, workshop words (sort, slip, hole, later). No exclamation marks.

## Motion
- Spatial map: Triage <-> Issues: crossfade (peer destinations). Issues -> Issue: drill in (slip rises 12px, 150ms); back reverses.
- Budget per screen type: Triage is used all day: slip enters 150ms, assign flight 140ms; nothing routine over 150ms. First load only: the case's six holes slide up in a 40ms stagger (hero 600ms total, once per session).
- Expressive moments (<=3): (1) the assign flight, the slip drops into the hole with `snappy`; (2) first-load case fill; (3) the SORTED stamp on an empty inbox (one scale-in from 1.15 to 1).
- Reduced motion: opacity and colour only; the flight becomes a 100ms fade.
